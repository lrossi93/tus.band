Privacy Policy

Last update: 10/09/2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the tus.band website (the "Site") handles data in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

1. Data controller

The Site is a project by Lorenzo Rossi, acting as the representative of the music project TÙS, which is not formally registered as a company or association.

For any request related to this Privacy Policy or to data processing, you can contact us at: info.tus.band@gmail.com

2. Data we process

The Site does not collect personal data through forms, contact forms, sign-ups, or user accounts, as none of these exist on the Site.

The only direct point of contact is the email address published on the "Booking" page (info.tus.band@gmail.com): if you write to us from there, the processing of any data you voluntarily provide (e.g. your email address, the content of your message) is governed by your own email provider`s privacy policy and, on our side, such data is used solely to reply to you and is not shared with third parties nor retained beyond what is necessary to handle the communication.

Automatically collected data (access logs)

Like any website, some technical data (e.g. IP address, browser type, pages visited, date and time of access) may be automatically logged by the infrastructure providers listed below, for security and technical operation purposes. We do not have direct access to these logs except in aggregated and anonymized form, where available.

3. Cookies

The Site does not use cookies of any kind — neither technical, nor profiling, nor third-party — and does not perform any form of tracking or statistical traffic analysis (for example, no Google Analytics or equivalent tool is installed).

Links to external services

The Site contains links to external platforms (e.g. Google Maps for directions, social media profiles) that open in a new tab. Once you click on such a link, navigation on that external site is governed by that platform`s own privacy policy, for which we are not responsible.

5. International data transfers

Some of the providers mentioned above (GitHub Inc., Google LLC) are based in the United States. Any transfer of technical data to countries outside the EU takes place under the safeguards provided by these vendors (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission).

6. Legal basis for processing

For the limited technical data processed (access logs for security and Site operation purposes), the legal basis is the legitimate interest of the Data Controller in ensuring the security and proper functioning of the Site (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

For data voluntarily provided via email, the legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual or contractual measures at your request, or your implied consent by initiating the communication (Art. 6.1.b/a GDPR).

7. Data retention

  • Technical log data managed by third-party providers is retained according to their respective policies (generally for limited periods, unless otherwise required by law).
  • Email communications are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and subsequently deleted, unless otherwise agreed with the data subject.

8. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request its rectification or erasure
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Object to processing
  • Request data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with your national Data Protection Authority (in Italy: www.garanteprivacy.it)

To exercise these rights, please write to: info.tus.band@gmail.com

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in how the Site operates or in applicable law. We encourage you to review it periodically. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this document.
